Heli Blast: Color Puzzle · FunBun Games

Questions, a bug, an idea for a level? Write to funbun.dev@gmail.com — we answer within two working days. Please mention your iPhone model, iOS version and the level number.

Frequently asked questions

I bought "Remove ads" but see ads again. Open the Shop tab (bottom bar, left) and tap "Restore purchases" at the bottom. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

Where is my progress stored? On your device. Deleting the game deletes the progress.

How do I stop personalised ads? In the game: Settings → Ad privacy choices. In iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

The leaderboard is empty. The world leaderboard uses Game Center — sign in under iOS Settings → Game Center.

A level is too hard. Use a booster: an extra pad, Rapid deployment or the golden Super helicopter. Every level can be solved.
